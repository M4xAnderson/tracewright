# The net vocabulary and the second explorer are crates of their own, and `net` holds no semantics

Amends ADR 0004. The workspace gains `crates/net`, `crates/recheck` and `crates/interop`. `net` holds the data types that both explorers speak (`Net`, `OpenNets`, `NetProperty`, `Marking`, `Counterexample`, `Evidence`, `Budget`, `NetVerdict`), their constructors, structural validation and one canonical encoding (`net::encode::canonical`, sorted by qualified ID). It holds no semantics: no firing rule, no capacity rule, no predicate evaluation and no composition of `OpenNets` into a `Net`. Those are implemented twice, once in `verify` (the main explorer) and once in `recheck` (own composition, plain breadth-first exploration, counterexample replay, certificate check), each written from the spec in ADR 0010 and ADR 0014, not from the other crate. `verify` and `recheck` depend only on `net`; `engine` owns lowering (Model to `OpenNets`) and flattening (`OpenNets` to `Net`), the Session, the check registry, undo history and the std adapters (`engine::adapters`: `StdDisk`, `FileJournal`, the notify watcher), so a CLI shares them with the Tauri app. `interop` holds the PNML writer for `net::Net`, used only by the development-time differential corpus for now. CI enforces the edges from `cargo metadata` (`recheck -/-> verify`, `recheck -/-> engine`, `verify -/-> recheck`, `verify -/-> model`, tokio and Tauri only in `app/src-tauri`) and a check that `net` has no `impl` block outside `encode.rs`, `validate.rs` and derives.

## Consequences

- A bug shared by both explorers can only sit in the data layout or the canonical encoding, which cannot make a transition enabled or a predicate true; a bug in either firing rule or composition shows up as "internal disagreement".
- Every rule in ADR 0010 and 0014 is written once as text and twice as code; a change to the spec changes both crates and their golden tests.
- The dependency rules of ADR 0004 otherwise hold: core crates use std threads and an atomic cancel flag, and the CLI sits on `engine`.
